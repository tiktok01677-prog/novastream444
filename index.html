Privacy & app info

Your math stays on your device. This version has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs or AI services. Expressions and practice answers are processed locally.

What is stored

Calculation history, bookmarks, practice progress, theme and angle settings are saved in this browser or WebView. Clear them in Saved & history. Clearing browser/app storage also removes them.

Website hosting

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting requests and may log IP addresses for security. Calculations are not sent to the host. GitHub privacy statement.

Supported mathematics

The equation solver handles real-coefficient linear and quadratic polynomials, including complex roots. The systems solver handles 2 or 3 linear equations. Graphs show sampled real-valued functions. Results use floating-point arithmetic and are displayed rounded; near-singular systems and extreme numbers may need higher-precision tools.
