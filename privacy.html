Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Our privacy approach

Hisaab is designed as a private, device-local expense planner. The app does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server that receives your transactions, budgets, goals or notes.

Information stored by the app

Information you enter—including transaction amounts, categories, accounts, notes, dates, budgets, savings goals, recurring rules, currency and appearance settings—is stored in local browser or WebView storage on your device.

Collection and sharing

Hisaab does not transmit your financial records to the developer, sell personal information, use advertising trackers or include analytics SDKs. The backup and CSV features create files only when you request them. You control where those files are saved or shared.

Website hosting

When the web version is hosted by a third-party provider such as GitHub Pages, that provider may process ordinary technical request data—such as an IP address, browser type and access time—under its own privacy terms. Hisaab does not add tracking to those requests.

Data retention and deletion

Your records remain on the device until you delete them, clear browser or app storage, or uninstall the Android app. You can use “Delete all my data” in Settings at any time. Clearing storage is permanent unless you previously exported a JSON backup.

Children's privacy

Hisaab is a general finance utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other user.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address displayed on Hisaab's Google Play store listing.

Important: If advertising, analytics, cloud sync or another third-party SDK is added in a future Android version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form must be updated before release.
