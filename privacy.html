1. About this policy

This policy describes the current AspireCompass Math Calculator & Solver web application and its calculator features when displayed in an Android WebView. These features include scientific calculations, equation solutions, complex-number calculations, function graphs, lessons and practice questions.

The current calculator version does not include accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, cloud synchronization or AI services. This policy describes that version’s data practices.

2. Information handled by the app

Mathematical inputs

Expressions, equations, coefficients, graph settings and practice answers are processed in your browser or WebView. The calculator does not send these inputs or their results to the publisher’s server.

Information saved on your device

  • Calculation and equation history, including results and timestamps.
  • Bookmarked results and saved graph intervals.
  • Practice-question attempts and mastery progress.
  • Preferences such as color theme and degree/radian mode.
  • App files cached for supported offline use.

These items are stored locally using browser or WebView storage. The app does not upload them or synchronize them across devices.

Privacy inquiries

If you choose to contact the publisher, the contact service and recipient may process the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and message. It is used to respond to your inquiry.

3. How information is used

Local information is used to calculate results, display solution steps and graphs, remember preferences, revisit calculations and show learning progress. Cached app files support offline access after a successful initial load where the browser or WebView supports it.

No behavioral tracking: the current calculator does not use advertising identifiers, profiling, analytics cookies or third-party tracking SDKs.

4. Website hosting and third parties

When AspireCompass is hosted on GitHub Pages, your browser or WebView contacts GitHub to download the app. GitHub may log the visitor’s IP address for security purposes. These hosting requests do not contain the expressions or practice answers entered in the calculator.

Hosting information is handled according to GitHub’s privacy statement. You can also read GitHub Pages’ data-collection explanation.

If you follow an external link or use a third-party contact service, that provider’s own privacy practices apply to the interaction.

5. Sharing and selling information

The calculator does not sell or transmit your mathematical inputs, local calculation history or learning progress to third parties. It does not contain advertisements in its current version.

Choosing Copy places the selected result on your device’s clipboard. If you then paste or share it using another application, that action is under your control and the other application’s practices apply.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved information remains in local storage until it is cleared, replaced or removed by the browser or device. Calculation history is limited to the latest 200 entries, so older entries may be replaced as new calculations are saved.

You can remove stored information in the following ways:

  1. Open Saved & history and delete an individual result or choose Clear history to remove calculations and bookmarks.
  2. Choose Reset practice progress to remove learning progress.
  3. Clear site data in your browser, or clear the Android app’s storage, to remove local settings, saved information and caches associated with that storage.

No account-deletion request is needed because this version has no user accounts. There is no publisher-operated cloud backup of your saved calculator information.

7. Device permissions and access

The calculator features do not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS messages or precise location. Internet access is used to load the hosted app and any external pages you choose to open.

Clipboard access is used only when you choose to copy a result. Browser or WebView storage is used to retain the local information described above.

8. Security

The hosted app should be accessed through its HTTPS address. Mathematical expressions are evaluated by a bounded parser rather than executed as JavaScript code. The publisher’s calculator does not need your password, payment information or identity documents.

Local storage is protected by your browser’s or device’s access controls. It is not a secure vault; people with access to your device or browser profile may be able to view saved results. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Students and children

The lessons and practice features can be used without submitting personal information or creating a profile. The current calculator does not request a user’s name, date of birth, school details or contact information, and does not track students through analytics or advertising SDKs.

A parent or guardian who has a privacy question can contact the publisher using the contact methods below.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when AspireCompass’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date. New practices, such as advertising, analytics or account features, will be described before they are introduced; consent will be requested where required.

11. Contact the publisher

Privacy contact: AspireCompass publisher

For the Android app, use the developer contact details provided on AspireCompass’s Google Play store listing. For the GitHub-hosted web app, contact the repository owner through that repository’s Issues page.

Please mention “AspireCompass Privacy” in your inquiry. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.
